01 The premise

IR systems do not stand still. Neither should their evaluation.

Collections evolve. User needs shift. Interaction signals accumulate. Models are updated. Yet information retrieval and recommendation systems are still often evaluated at isolated snapshots.

EvolveIR brings together research on systems that operate under continuous change—connecting temporal retrieval, adaptive recommendation, evolving user and relevance models, feedback loops, changing knowledge, RAG, and responsible evaluation.

Our central question “How should information retrieval and recommendation systems adapt and be evaluated when users, information, interactions, knowledge, and models evolve over time?”

02 Topics of interest

Research that sees
the whole timeline.

We welcome methodological, empirical, resource, and position contributions. Topics include, but are not limited to:

01

Evolving retrieval

  • Temporal and continual IR
  • Changing collections and relevance
  • Online and continual learning to rank
  • Session-based information access
02

Users & recommendation

  • Temporal recommender systems
  • Evolving preferences and personalisation
  • User-system feedback loops
  • Behavioural dynamics and drift
03

Knowledge & generation

  • Dynamic knowledge graphs
  • RAG over evolving corpora
  • Updating, memory, and forgetting
  • Knowledge-enhanced retrieval
04

Evaluation & responsibility

  • Longitudinal evaluation protocols
  • Robustness and stability over time
  • Fairness, exposure, and diversity
  • Transparency and user agency
